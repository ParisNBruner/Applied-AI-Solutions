# Applied AI Solutions

This repository is a collection of practical AI use cases, solutions, and proof of concepts I’ve developed to address business challenges, improve workflows, and support teams in their day-to-day work.

Each project highlights how I’ve explored and applied AI to solve real-world problems, support colleagues across different teams and engagements, and identify opportunities to make work more efficient and effective.

The focus is on practical applications of AI, translating business needs into accessible, usable solutions.
